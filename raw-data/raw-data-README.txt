bioassay-leaves-initial
Description of data: For each plant, area of the second-newest fully expanded leaf as estimated from photos using LeafByte.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data from 'Sheet1' of 2022-08-27_USDA-cotton_bioassay-after2.xlsx

bioassay-leaves-final
Description of data: For each plant, area of each half-leaf used in bioassays after 48 h of Spodoptera exigua feeding, estimated from photos using LeafByte.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data from 'Sheet1' of 2022-10-26_USDA-cotton_bioassay-after2.xlsx

bioassay-RGR-data
Description of data: For each bioassay caterpillar (2 per plant), mass before and after 48 h no-choice bioassay feeding period (measured after 2 h starvation period for gut clearance), bioassay start and end times, and relative growth rate calculated as (ln(final mass) - ln(initial mass))/days elapsed.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data from 'bioassay RGR data' tab of 2022-08-24_USDA-cotton_bioassay-data-sheet.xlsx

chem-data-formatted
Description of data: For each plant, relative abundances of two isomers of hemigossypolone (3.6 and 10.5 retention times) as detected using mass spectrometry.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'formatted-JM' tab of Hemigossypolone-allSamples.xlsx

leaf-trait-midvein-lengths
Description of data: Midvein lengths for the leaf samples used to estimate trichome densities, terpenoid gland densities, and hemigossypolone relative abundances. 
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'midvein-lengths' tab of 2024-05-29_USDA-cotton_physical-leaf-trait-data.xlsx

phys-traits-by-observer
Description of data: Subset of leaf images where trichome and terpenoid gland densities were measured by more than one observer, for standardizing these estimates across observers.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'traits-by-obs' tab of 2024-05-29_USDA-cotton_physical-leaf-trait-data.xlsx

phys-trait-data
Description of data: For each plant, (unstandardized) estimates of trichome and terpenoid gland densities within each of two 5 mm diameter leaf discs.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'phys-traits' tab of 2024-05-29_USDA-cotton_physical-leaf-trait-data.xlsx

plant-guide
Description of data: For each plant, cage position in the field (i.e., plot ID), experimental treatment (herbivore density and timepoint of leaf sampling), and genotype (accession ID).
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'all-plants' tab of 2022-06-18_USDA-cotton_geno-assignments-actual.xlsx

terpenoid-gland-areas
Description of data: Total area taken up by terpenoid glands, within each of two 5 mm diameter leaf discs per plant. Determined using the 'Analyze Particles' function with binarized images in ImageJ, with one observer (JM) manually adjusting the color level for each photo.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 2024-05-09_USDA-cotton_gland-areas.csv

field-obs-data
Description of data: For each plant, damage estimates (visually estimated from 10 leaves by the same observer, JM) before adding caterpillars, at the time of leaf sampling, and at the end of the experiment. Also includes initial size measurements (summed length of all stems and branches > 4 cm) and categorical estimate of aphid density.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'field obs' tab of 2022-10-21_USDA-cotton_data.xlsx
** 2025-07-07: corrected initial stem length for 6515 in 'field-obs-data' (typo in sum)

final-biomass
Description of data: Estimates of final dry weight after plants were harvested at the end of the experiment.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'final-biomass' tab of 2022-10-21_USDA-cotton_data.xlsx

stem-lengths-to-biomass
Description of data: For a subset of plants, size measurements (summed length of all stems and branches > 4 cm) and final dry weight at the time of harvest.
**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'plant size & biomass' tab of 2022-10-21_USDA-cotton_data.xlsx

**Note: .csv in USDA-cotton-exp/raw-data saved from 'all-plants' tab of 2022-06-18_USDA-cotton_geno-assignments-actual.xlsx
